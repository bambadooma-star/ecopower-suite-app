Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and aim for complete transparency. This policy describes how EcoPower Suite and this website handle data, and our compliance with the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (5741-1981).

Effective date: November 2026 · Applies to v1.1.4

SummaryIsraeli lawGDPRCCPACookies & storageHosting

Executive summary: no telemetry, local-first

The EcoPower Suite desktop application collects no personal data and sends no telemetry. There are no accounts, tracking pixels or analytics. It manages your Windows power plans and monitor brightness through local system APIs, and its settings and savings history are stored only on your computer in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EcoPowerSuite\config.json.

The one network exception: the app can contact GitHub (api.github.com and github.com) to check for and download a newer release. The app checks at launch and every 6 hours, and installs a verified update automatically (updates are always on). Like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and a request header identifying the updater (EcoPowerSuite-Updater) under GitHub's own privacy statement. EcoPower Suite does not add any identifier of its own.

3. European Union GDPR (Regulation EU 2016/679)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights under Articles 15–22 of the GDPR:

  • Access (Art. 15): ask whether personal data about you is processed.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): ask for inaccurate data to be corrected.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): because the data this site keeps lives only in your own browser, clearing your browser's local storage (or using Clear / reset data in the Page stats dialog) erases it instantly.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) & portability (Art. 20): export your local page stats as JSON from the Page stats dialog.
  • Objection (Art. 21): object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with an EU supervisory authority in your member state.

Legal basis: to the extent any processing occurs (for example, GitHub Pages server logs), it relies on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in operating and securing a static website.

5. Cookies & Local Storage (ePrivacy Directive)

This website sets no cookies — no third-party tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. It stores the following strictly functional keys in your browser's sandboxed storage, on your device only:

  • eco_theme, eco_lang: your theme and language choice.
  • eco_a11y: your accessibility settings (text size, contrast, link highlight, paused motion, readable font).
  • eco_privacy_notice_dismissed: remembers that you closed the privacy notice.
  • ecopower_telemetry_store: anonymous page counters (visits, referrer hostnames, simulator/calculator use, download clicks) kept only in your browser and never transmitted. You can view, export or erase it from Page stats in the footer.
  • eco_session_active, eco_gh_count (session storage): a per-tab flag and a 10-minute cache of the public GitHub download count.

You can clear these at any moment through your browser settings or the Clear / reset data button in the Page stats dialog.

6. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Neither the website nor the application is directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in certain EU member states). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Hosting & Third-Party Services

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft Corporation). GitHub may collect standard HTTP server logs (IP address, user-agent, request headers) for operational integrity and DDoS defense under GitHub's Global Privacy Statement.

All styles, scripts, fonts and icons are served from this site itself — there are no third-party CDNs, fonts, analytics or ad scripts. The only third-party request the page makes is a public, unauthenticated call to the GitHub Releases API to display the download count; GitHub sees your IP address for that request. Links to VirusTotal, PayPal and social networks only load if you click them.
