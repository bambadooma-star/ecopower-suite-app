Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency. This policy outlines our strict offline principles and compliance with the European Union GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (5741-1981).

Executive Summary: Zero Telemetry & 100% Offline Software

The EcoPower Suite desktop application operates 100% offline. It contains no spyware, no background telemetry, no tracking pixels, and does not open any network sockets to external servers. It manages your Windows power schemes and monitor brightness purely through local system APIs.

3. European Union GDPR Compliance (Regulation EU 2016/679)

If you are an individual resident within the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific statutory rights under Articles 15 through 22 of the GDPR:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request confirmation of whether your personal data is processed.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure / "To Be Forgotten" (Article 17): Request deletion of personal data. (Because our software runs 100% offline, clearing your browser’s local storage completely erases all website telemetry instantly).
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18) & Data Portability (Article 20): Export your telemetry metrics via the built-in JSON exporter.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with an EU data protection supervisory authority in your member state.

Legal Basis for Processing: We rely on Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) to maintain website security, prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) abuse via GitHub Pages infrastructure, and optimize software compatibility across hardware platforms.

5. Cookie & Local Storage (ePrivacy Directive)

EcoPower Suite uses ZERO third-party tracking cookies and ZERO advertising cookies. We believe in an uncluttered web without deceptive cookie walls.

We store the following strictly functional keys inside your browser’s sandboxed localStorage:

  • ecopower_telemetry_store: Retains anonymous visitor counts, screen resolution, and simulator interactions on your own device.
  • eco_session_active: Session flag to prevent duplicate visit counting during page refreshes.
  • eco_accessibility_settings: Remembers your high-contrast and text-size preferences.

You may clear these keys at any moment via your browser settings or by clicking "Clear / Reset Data" in the internal console.
