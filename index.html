Frequently Asked Questions

How does laptop battery auto-switching work?

When you unplug your laptop from AC wall power, EcoPower Suite detects the transition via Windows Power APIs and automatically switches your power profile to Balanced mode. This avoids aggressive 10% screen dimming while preventing heavy CPU turbo boost power drains on battery.

Will watching movies or playing games get interrupted?

No. The built-in Smart Inactivity Shield constantly tracks Windows audio playback streams, fullscreen window states, and connected gamepads (Xbox/PlayStation via XInput). If any of these are active, auto-idle dimming is paused.

Does it support multiple monitors?

Yes! EcoPower Suite enumerates all active monitors connected via HDMI, DisplayPort, or USB-C and applies DDC/CI hardware brightness commands across all compatible external displays simultaneously, in addition to laptop internal screens via WMI.

How does it start with Windows?

It registers directly into HKCU\...\CurrentVersion\Run with the --minimized flag. When your PC boots, it starts silently in the System Tray (notification area near the clock) without popping up any distracting windows.
