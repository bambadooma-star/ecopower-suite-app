Everything in one tray app

Smarter power, zero babysitting

EcoPower Suite applies changes as clean one-shot events — it never fights you with background loops — and lives quietly in your system tray.

Three smart power modes

Eco Saver caps the CPU, dims screens and enables PCIe ASPM max savings. Balanced is the everyday default. Max Performance unlocks full turbo and zero bus latency.

Automatic idle saver

After 2–60 minutes of inactivity the PC moves to Eco, then restores your previous mode the moment you touch the mouse or keyboard. Prefer to stay in control? Lock Eco manually.

Every screen, one slider

Controls external monitors over DDC/CI and laptop panels over WMI at the same time, with correct range scaling per monitor and non-blocking updates.

Smart Inactivity Shield

Never dims during a video call, movie, game or local-AI job. Audio, fullscreen, gamepad and AI-workload detection pause the idle timer automatically.

Scheduled shutdown

Daily shutdown via Windows Task Scheduler with a 30-second warning, a real one-hour snooze, or cancel-for-today. Stop paying for a PC that runs all night.

Savings tracker

Track kWh and money saved today, year-to-date and projected for the next 12 months, in your currency and at your own electricity rate.

Universal hardware coverage

Built to run on every PC you own

A high-end gaming rig, a dual-screen workstation or an ultraportable on battery — the same single executable.

Desktop PCs

VESA DDC/CI control over HDMI and DisplayPort, plus lower idle draw from motherboards and discrete GPUs.

Laptops & notebooks

Multi-monitor setups

False-positive prevention

The Smart Inactivity Shield

Standard Windows timers dim your screen mid-movie or mid-call. EcoPower Suite reads native Windows audio, fullscreen and controller signals so you are never interrupted.

  • Fullscreen video & media detection

    YouTube, Netflix, Disney+, VLC and presentations running fullscreen on any monitor.

  • Active audio stream monitoring

    A native IAudioSessionManager2 listener keeps the PC awake during Spotify, Zoom, Discord or Teams calls.

  • Game controller activity (XInput)

    Playing with an Xbox or PlayStation pad and no mouse? Controller input keeps the PC active.

  • AI & local-LLM workload shield

    Tracks local inference (Ollama, LM Studio, ComfyUI, llama.cpp, Whisper) and NVIDIA CUDA compute above 15% load, so idle sleep and CPU capping never interrupt token generation or diffusion renders.

  • Bot & AI-agent CLI automation

    A headless CLI with JSON output (--status --json, --mode <eco|balanced|perf>, --brightness <0-100>) lets scripts and agents control power schemes programmatically.

Frequently asked questions

How does laptop battery auto-switching work?
When you unplug from AC power, EcoPower Suite detects the transition through the Windows power API and switches once to Balanced mode. That avoids aggressive 10% dimming while preventing heavy turbo-boost drain on battery.
Will watching movies or playing games get interrupted?
No. The Smart Inactivity Shield tracks Windows audio streams, fullscreen windows, gamepads (XInput) and local AI workloads. While any of them is active, auto-idle dimming is paused.
Does it support multiple monitors?
Yes. It enumerates every monitor on HDMI, DisplayPort or USB-C and sends DDC/CI brightness commands (scaled to each monitor's real range) in addition to laptop internal screens via WMI.
How does it start with Windows?
If enabled in Settings, it registers under HKCU\…\CurrentVersion\Run with the --minimized flag and starts silently in the system tray. You can turn it off at any time.
Does it connect to the internet?
Not for anything it does day to day. Only to check for and download updates from GitHub. The check runs at launch and every 6 hours, and the update is installed automatically after it is verified. There is no telemetry.
Will it change my Windows settings?
It switches between Windows power plans and sets CPU, PCIe link-state and screen-timeout values on them, plus monitor brightness. Exit & Restore in Settings switches back to Balanced and restores your brightness.
Is it free? Is it open source?
It is free to use with no ads, supported by voluntary donations. It is proprietary closed-source software, so you can't read its code — which is why we publish checksums and encourage scanning the file yourself.
How do I uninstall it?
There is no installer. Open Settings → Exit and restore. If you set a scheduled shutdown, turn it off first on the Auto Shutdown page (the schedule is kept even after the file is deleted), then delete the .exe. You can also turn off "Start with Windows".
